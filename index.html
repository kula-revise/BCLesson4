The argument so far

Four moves. This is now a whole essay in outline, and every one of them answers the word 'only':

1. Political liberation was one focus, because after 1960 there was no legal party, no vote and no citizenship.

2. But the starting diagnosis was psychological: liberation had to begin in the mind.

3. And the movement acted on economic and social conditions, building clinics, funds and unions the state would not build.

4. In the schools the fronts fused, and the state's own response in 1977 showed what it had become.

In Lesson 5 you assemble it into the answer, one paragraph at a time, against the official answer sheet the markers were given.
